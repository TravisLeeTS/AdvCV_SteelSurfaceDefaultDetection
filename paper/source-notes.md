# Sources for the experimental exhibits

The study evaluates the one-to-one detection head in YOLOv10 and the partial-convolution idea in FasterNet on six NEU-DET steel defect classes. Full bibliographic entries for the exhibits are in [references.bib](references.bib). [YOLOv10 proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/c34ddd05eb089991f06f3c5dc36836e0-Abstract.html) · [FasterNet paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Chen_Run_Dont_Walk_Chasing_Higher_FLOPS_for_Faster_Neural_Networks_CVPR_2023_paper.html) · [NEU dataset authors](https://faculty.neu.edu.cn/songkc/en/zdylm/263265/)

The course survey by Lee Ting Sen and Khalifa Alshamsi motivated the resolution, latency and suppression questions. The measured follow-up is in [survey_evidence.json](../artifacts/study/report/survey_evidence.json).
